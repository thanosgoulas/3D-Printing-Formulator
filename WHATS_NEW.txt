THE 3D PRINTING FORMULATOR — What's New in v1.1
================================================

1. Bug fixes
   Various bug fixes and stability improvements.

2. Blend breakdown tooltip
   After clicking Solve or Calculate, hovering over a blend
   component in the results table now shows the individual
   constituent masses calculated from the total batch mass.

3. Merge DB
   A new Merge DB button has been added to the Materials
   Database editor. Use it when upgrading to a new version
   to add new default materials without affecting your
   existing or custom database entries.

4. Corrected material properties

5. New materials added
